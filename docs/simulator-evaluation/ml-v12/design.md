This separately frozen follow-up responds to the failed v11 experiment. V11's online union added a false alert without a detection over its CatBoost component. V11's recent-account follow-up gained four detections with no extra false alerts. Neither alone met the five-point pooled recall gate. Their results remain unchanged, including all failures.

Three bounded candidates: combine recent-account follow-up with the historical CatBoost online detector; extend that online detector with a causal, EBM-corroborated account episode; combine both extensions. Reuse v11 calibration and frozen historical models. The historical CatBoost's online alerts seed the online episode; v10's recent EBM cannot seed online episodes when it finds no online fraud. Restrict additions to online purchases on the same account, strictly after an original predicted seed and within 1/24/168 hours. Require a CatBoost score within 4/8 raw units of its cutoff and a separately calibrated historical EBM score. These score scales belong to the same frozen model within each comparison; scores from different families are not averaged.

Previously rescued alerts cannot seed either extension; online and recent-account extensions do not recursively seed each other. Current labels never enter inference.
