# Repair numerical instability before judging model quality

The v7 2016 EBM and its miner produced enormous finite logits (approximately 1e45 and 1e26), with divergent interaction terms. The previous finite-only check did not catch this. Comparisons against those collapsed policies and claims about hard-negative mining are invalid. Unweighted v7 CatBoost models are unaffected. Checked v4 pilot score magnitudes and v5/v6 package terms do not show this failure; earlier reported results are not retroactively invalidated.

Use bounded leaf updates (`max_delta_step=1`) and L2 regularization (`reg_lambda=1`) for every new EBM, including miners. Reject raw logits, intercepts or term values with nonfinite values or absolute magnitude above 1000, before mining, exporting or evaluating. This conservative numerical health limit is not probability calibration; do not clip a failed model into looking valid. InterpretML documents these parameters in its [classifier API](https://interpret.ml/docs/python/api/ExplainableBoostingClassifier.html).

Preserve v7 and prior artifacts. V8 is a new, explicitly exploratory experiment using the same three rolling periods and sampled training populations. Compare repaired original-capacity soft EBM, stronger soft EBM, then the stronger model with sequence/merchant context, geography, delayed feedback and hard-negative weighting. Every main method averages two seeds. The stronger EBM has 512 rounds, 15 interactions and learning rate .03; the repaired baseline keeps 256 rounds, five interactions and .05. All EBMs receive the same stability controls.

Use the 700-tree depth-eight soft CatBoost as the conventional comparator. The identical, unaffected 2016 models may be reused only after training identities, labels and sampling weights reconcile. Fit the same fixed configuration on later periods. No second all-CatBoost feature sweep is necessary while repairing the demonstrated failure. Only the stronger EBM ablations isolate new information at fixed model settings.

Every fraud is retained; deterministic hash-sampled legitimate rows receive inverse inclusion weights. Histories and evaluation populations remain complete. Mature merchant feedback uses fit-role labels only and the already declared 30-day delay assumption. Select cutoffs on the calibration quarter at >=50% recall; freeze before evaluation. No new untouched-test claim is made, including for periods already read in v7.

Report all candidates, support, numeric health, accuracy/precision/recall/F1, AP, 1% review metrics, channel results and six paired-user comparisons against repaired soft EBM with Bonferroni-adjusted intervals. Advancement requires +3pp precision, positive adjusted precision lower bound, recall noninferiority lower bound above -5pp, every-fold recall >=40%, and at least 100 frauds/20 positive users pooled. The full-data latest-fold follow-through and frozen old-model online-routing probe are descriptive after selection. No runtime promotion follows automatically. Neural pretraining remains a later experiment; fixing invalid training takes precedence.
