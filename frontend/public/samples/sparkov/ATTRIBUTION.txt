Sparkov simulated credit-card transactions
Dataset publisher: Kartik Shenoy (kartik2112)
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/kartik2112/fraud-detection
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain), per the dataset publisher.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

These files are curated subsets of fraudTest.csv from dataset version 1.
They are simulated transactions, not bank customer records.
Original amounts, times, categories, merchant identities and outcome labels are preserved.
UTC and USD are import conventions; account and merchant display aliases are added.
Demographics, names, addresses, customer location and other unused fields are omitted.
No transaction is omitted inside a selected account-cohort window.
Selection used current-model performance; these are demonstrations, not independent validation.
